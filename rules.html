Rules and FAQs

Hostel rules

  1. Smoking is not allowed in the hostel.
  2. Visitors are allowed only in the visiting area and only during visiting hours.
  3. Keep the kitchen, dining area and laundry clean after use.
  4. Cooking is allowed only in the shared kitchen.
  5. Report any damage to hostel staff at once.
  6. Monthly rent is paid before the 5th of each month.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compare rooms online?

Yes. See the Rooms page.

How do I apply?

Send an enquiry on the Enquire page. The hostel will reply.

Is there online payment?

No. This website does not take payments or bookings.
